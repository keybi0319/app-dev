# app-dev

**Sakamoto Days is a fast-paced action comedy manga about a retired hitman trying to live a peaceful life—but his past won’t let him go.** 

*Taro Sakamoto*  was once the most feared assassin in the underworld, unmatched in skill and ruthlessness. But everything changed when he fell in love, got married, and became a father. Now overweight and running a humble convenience store, Sakamoto is determined to leave violence behind. However, his old life keeps catching up with him—rivals, bounty hunters, and secret organizations refuse to believe he’s truly retired.

Despite his laid-back appearance:
- **Sakamoto Days** delivers thrilling battles, clever humor, and heartfelt moments as Sakamoto uses his deadly skills to protect his family and friends—all while trying not to kill anyone.
- The series blends slapstick comedy with intense action, making it a standout in the shōnen genre
